# Feedback & Community Testing

Akad's feedback loop for the Rise In × Midnight "New Moon to Full" builder program: real people try the live app, then report back through a short form, so usage and feedback are verifiable rather than just claimed.

## Try the app

[akad-dzakwannajmis-projects.vercel.app](https://akad-dzakwannajmis-projects.vercel.app) — connect a Midnight wallet (1AM recommended), claim free AKD from the built-in faucet, and try a public swap, wrap to private, a private swap, and unwrap. No real funds needed, it's all on Midnight testnet (Preview or Preprod, selectable in-app).

## Give feedback

[forms.gle/dfwnHbQKNsZtm6DF7](https://forms.gle/dfwnHbQKNsZtm6DF7)

Takes under a minute. It asks for your wallet address, which network you tested on, which feature(s) you tried, a transaction hash from your own session (so a response is tied to real, checkable usage rather than an unverifiable claim), a 1-5 rating, and optional open feedback.
